Hey there! I'm Nick Pattas, a VFX Technical Artist living in Athens, Greece.

I have experience working in small indie teams to large-scale cinema and TV productions. Basically, every type of screen and medium is my playground.

I'm proficient in Unreal Engine 5 workflows and systems (Niagara, Materials, Blueprints, PCG), but my passion for the craft does not limited by the engine I'm using.

Not a programmer for sure, but I'm more than willing to get my hands dirty with code, from Unreal's C++ and HLSL to custom Python scripts for making awesome tools.

On this website, you can find some of my personal projects that I work on in my free time, with as detailed a breakdown as possible.

Always looking to collaborate with passionate people on crazy projects, so don't hesitate to reach out.
