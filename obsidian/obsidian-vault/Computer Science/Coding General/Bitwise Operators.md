Bitwise operators are fast and simple operations on **binary data** where each binary digit **treated individually**. 
A bitwise operation operates on a **bit string**, a **bit array** or a **binary number** at the level of its **individual bits**.

*On simple low-cost processors, typically, bitwise operations are substantially faster than division, several times faster than multiplication, and sometimes significantly faster than addition.*

Bitwise operations are necessary particularly in lower-level programming such as device drivers, low-level graphics, communications protocol packet assembly, and decoding.
## Bit Masking
Bit masking is the process of getting the processor to **ignore** all the bits that we don't want to work on and **only process the digits we want to process**.
