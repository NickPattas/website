# Motion Capture Systems

[[Motion Capture]]

Gait analysis is the measurement of body kinematics and dynamics.

Kinematic gait analysis is often based on markers located according to a standard arrangement (marker set). A variety of sets have been proposed but the majority in clinical use are based on some variation of the Helen Hayes (HH) set. 

1. The basic laboratory system for optical based gait analysis called [[Sterophotogrammetric Motion Capture Systems]]. Such systems include high-end infrared cameras systems (VICON, OptiTrack) considered gold-standards for motion analysis research.
2. Recent approaches in gait analysis is the use of motion sensors based on [[Inertial Measurement Units (IMU)]] (IMUs) (Xsens, Rokoko). HTC Vive Trackers (6DOF tracking) have also used in order to develop low-cost motion analysis systems.
3. Another image processing method is [[Homography]] wich relates the transformation between two planes. Such [[Augmented Reality]] systems are usually based on open-source solutions such as OpenCV.
4. Markerless motion capture system has also been proposed such as OpenPose, Kinect sensors etc.

*The pandemic has highlighted the need for home-based use, portable, budget and user friendly motion capture solutions.
